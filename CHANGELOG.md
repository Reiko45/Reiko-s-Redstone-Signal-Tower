# Changelog

Each version's section is uploaded to CurseForge as that file's changelog, so add one for every new version (the heading is `## ` and the version number).

## 0.68.1

- First release on CurseForge.
- The mod is now called **Reiko's Wireless Redstone** (it was Reiko's Redstone Signal Tower). Only the name changed: worlds made with earlier versions keep all their blocks, items and frequencies.
- The jar is now named `reikos_wireless_redstone-<version>.jar`. Remove the old `reikos_signal_tower-….jar` from your mods folder, or the game finds the mod twice and won't start.

## 0.68.0

- **Security Console:** a wall panel that watches up to 8 zones (Instant, Delayed or Always), armed and disarmed by a keypad or remote on its own frequency or from its screen, with exit and entry delays and an "Arm at night" schedule. When the alarm goes off it turns its alarm frequency on for 2 minutes, sounds a siren, sends "ALARM: Front Door (Alex)" to pagers and walkie-talkies, and switches TV Monitors on the alarm frequency to the camera on the tripped zone. It keeps a log of the last 32 events.
- **Door Sensor:** turns its frequency on while the door, trapdoor, fence gate, barrel or chest next to it is open, naming who opened it.
- Fixed: a Radio Drone could keep its pilot after losing the pilot's signal.
