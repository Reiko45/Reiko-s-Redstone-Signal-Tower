# CurseForge gallery

Upload these on the project's **Images** tab, in this order, with these titles and descriptions. The files are in `docs/images/`. Mark the first one as featured.

1. `towers.png`. **Radio towers.** A Radio Transmitter on top of a column of Radio Masts broadcasts the redstone signal it gets; the higher it stands, the farther it reaches.
2. `repeater-tower.png`. **Repeater towers.** A repeater on a mast relays a frequency with its own reach, lighting a lamp 130 blocks from the transmitter, and the Signal Scanner points the way to it.
3. `tv.png`. **TV Cameras and video walls.** A 3 × 2 wall of TV Monitors showing a camera's live picture, the camera on its stand, and its settings.
4. `drone.png`. **Radio Drone.** Flying the drone through its camera, carrying hay bales, with the monitor wall showing its picture.
5. `security.png`. **Security System.** The Security Console in alarm: a Door Sensor names who opened the door, the video wall switches to the camera on that zone, and pagers get the alarm.
6. `radar.png`. **Radar.** A Radar Screen showing the players, mobs and vehicles a Radar Dish sees, and the dish turning on its mast.
7. `satellite-uplink.png`. **Satellites.** A rocket lifts a satellite into orbit; a tower with a Satellite Dish is then heard everywhere in the dimension.
8. `old-radio-towers-and-climbing.png`. **Old radio towers.** An abandoned tower found in a taiga with its hut and guy wires, and climbing its mast to the platform under the top.
9. `signal-fade-and-dish-antenna.png`. **Signal fade and the Dish Antenna.** Lamps dimming toward the edge of a tower's reach, and a Dish Antenna beaming three times as far one way.
10. `jade-tooltips.png`. **Jade tooltips.** What a transmitter, repeater, mast and receiver show in Jade: frequency, signal and reach.
